# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Listen after the first chapter**: episodes are planned as chapters, then written and voiced one chapter at a time. Chapter 1 is short, so playback starts as soon as it is voiced (about as long as the old wait for a 3-minute episode), and chapter k+1 is written while chapter k is voiced and heard. The audio streams into the player (MediaSource), keeps at most three minutes buffered ahead so even 30 minutes stays within browser limits, and waits at the edge with "the next chapter is almost ready" if voicing ever falls behind. Browsers without streaming playback get the finished episode as before. A document read once is never read again: its brief is cached on the device, so another length or language starts at chapter 1.
- **Three lengths: 8, 15 and 30 minutes** (the key points, a close reading, a deep reading). Older choices map to the nearest; "Too long" / "Want more" move one length. On the free trial, lengths today's allowance can't cover are offered only with your own keys. Self-evolution replays long episodes at 8 minutes, so tuning stays cheap.
- **You can see it working**: the wait shows real progress streamed from the model (characters written, not a guess), a clock, and a breathing dot on the current step; the bar keeps drifting through the model's silent reading time instead of freezing. This is the one place anything moves.
- **No prompt to fill in**: the focus line is gone from Create. The prefill writes it from the document and what it knows about you (it now sees your past focus lines too), and lenses and quality mode are set the same way; "Show everything" brings the controls back.
- **A player that stays put**: Now Playing is one fixed page. Title and chapter on top; artwork, chapters (jump to any; the ones still being made say so) and rating on the left; on the right, a visual and a transcript window that keeps the spoken line in the middle. The page itself never scrolls. Chapter marks sit on the scrubber, with the voiced part shaded while it's still being made. Space plays and pauses, arrows skip. On a phone it's the same single screen: visual, transcript, transport.
- **Figures and sketches beside the narration**: figures and tables are found in dropped PDFs (by their captions, cropped from the rendered page, all on the device) and shown while the hosts discuss them. Each chapter also gets simple drawings written with the script: the steps of a method, numbers side by side, a tiny worked example or a key term, in the episode's language. Episodes without visuals show their takeaways there.
- **Voices for the topic, in the flow**: with your own paid ElevenLabs key, the voice menu on Create offers three host pairs ElevenLabs recommends for this kind of document (its educational picks for papers and reports), each with a preview of both voices. Picking one is remembered for that show and language.

- **A key that can't speak is caught up front**: ElevenLabs keys can be limited to chosen permissions, and one without *Text to Speech* used to pass sign-up ("Key works · Eleven v4") and then fail only after the script was paid for. Sign-up now checks for free (an empty-text request: nothing is spoken or billed) and says exactly which permission to turn on; every run checks again before spending, and Settings shows the problem instead of "Connected". Permission errors anywhere (voice picker, intro sting) read in your language and name the switch to flip, and are no longer retried.
- **Every mode speaks Eleven v4**: Lite used Flash v2.5 while the app said Eleven v4; per-line modes now use Eleven v4 Turbo, the same price. The Engine card shows the model each mode really uses.
- **The trial no longer overwrites your voices**: it speaks from another ElevenLabs account where your own voices don't exist, so a stand-in voice is used for that run only, and the line under the title says so.
- **Smaller fixes from clicking every button**: ±15 s / +30 s add up when tapped quickly; Escape closes an open menu without closing the player; a Chinese episode's Takeaways no longer lead with the English brief; the intro-sting preview no longer pushes Settings' switches out of their card; saving a designed voice no longer says "Designing…"; Lite's progress line no longer says it verifies; the mini player and the library show the same length.

- **It gets better with use, on its own and safely**: with your own keys, after 5 new episodes or 2 negative signals (a thumbs‑down, or deleting an episode before hearing most of it) Evocast runs one small tuning step for the medium harness while you listen to the episode you just made: it measures the current version again, analyzes your recent episodes, makes one balanced plan and tries it on 2 of your episodes, and switches only if the result passes at least 5 points more tests at no more than +25% cost (or is 20% cheaper with no fewer passed). It stays under a weekly budget ($1 by default; change it or turn it off in You, where what it spent this week is shown), never runs on the free trial (the server refuses it too), and picks up where it stopped if the tab closes. Home says what changed in one line ("Tuned from your last 5 episodes: tighter turns, ~1 min shorter · Undo").
- **A live guard for content changes**, like the interface's: after any epoch switches a mode's harness, its next 3 episodes are compared with the previous version's last 3, scored on seconds actually heard, rating, deletions, the rule pass rate and delivered vs requested length. If they land clearly worse, the previous version comes back and Home says so. Deleted episodes now leave their outcome behind so a deletion still counts.
- **Measurements are reused only when they still apply**: cached validation metrics are keyed by the model and a hash of the listener notes as well, so switching models or learning a new preference measures again.
- **Fewer model calls when your habits are clear**: when your last 6 choices would have been pre‑filled correctly from habit alone, the content‑aware prefill call is skipped.
- **The evolution engine's controls move behind "Show everything"**; You shows only the one‑line budget. Deleting an episode now waits for its listening data to be saved first, so a late save can't bring it back.

- **Calm by design**: nothing moves any more. Gone: springs, slides, the spinning rainbow glow, the cursor spotlight, magnetic and press-scale buttons, hover lifts, page transitions and staggered lists; things that appear only fade, briefly. Fewer controls: the player's four speed buttons become one, the mini player is one target, "Pin as default", the "Usual" chips, the library language filter, the extra onboarding step and the duplicate reset are gone; Settings shows voices only with an ElevenLabs key and keeps rarely used switches under Advanced. Touch targets reach 44px, and secondary text meets contrast guidelines in light mode.
- **It learns from what you actually do**: listening counts seconds really played (seeks no longer inflate it), plus jumps back, skipped seconds and where you stopped; the evolution analyzer reads them as "stopped at line 14/30". Rating an episode no longer overwrites its listening data, "Too long" / "Want more" now change the next length, a feedback tap no longer erases your name and portrait summary, and only finished episodes teach the prefill. After each episode the prefill re-tunes itself from your past choices when that clearly helps, and says so in one line.
- **Fixed tests are fixed**: every harness is graded against the seed's length, turn and reaction thresholds, so evolution can no longer pass by writing a shorter episode than you asked for. Content evolution is off on the free trial (an epoch costs more than a day's allowance) with a one-line pointer to adding keys.
- **Voices**: the trial meters ElevenLabs speech and music at the Gateway's live prices (the launch discount ends; without a live price it counts the full list price), and trial intro stings are instrumental at the voices' sample rate. Per-line voices get their neighbouring lines as context again, documented long audio cues are recognised and never read aloud, `[pause]` gives way to ellipses, and alpha-only models are never auto-picked.
- **Model detection cross-checks the catalogs**: the suggested model is the newest flagship the key can use, chosen from release dates and prices cross-checked across OpenRouter, Vercel AI Gateway and models.dev (median of the catalogs; models listed by only one aren't suggested). The cheapest and most capable are labelled, each model shows its price, and the chosen model's real price feeds cost estimates. No more hard-coded model families going stale: an OpenAI key now gets `gpt-6.1-sol`, not `gpt-5.5`.
- **20+ providers**: Xiaomi MiMo, MiniMax, Volcengine Doubao, SiliconFlow, StepFun, Tencent Hunyuan, Baidu Qianfan, xAI, Mistral, Groq, Together, Fireworks, Perplexity, Cerebras and Vercel AI Gateway join the list. Key shapes follow the providers' docs and secret-scanner rules, including Gemini's new `AQ.` keys, MiniMax `sk-api-`, MiMo Token Plan `tp-` and DashScope `sk-ws` / `sk-sp-`. A provider's China, international and token-plan endpoints are tried in turn; keys are still never tried on another company's API. Keys Evocast can't use (Kimi Code, Perplexity, admin keys) say why instead of failing vaguely.
- **Voices that work on every plan**: free ElevenLabs plans can't use Voice Library voices through the API, so each seat now defaults to the closest voice in the account (by language, gender and use), read once at sign-up and named in the onboarding hint. An account with no voices gets one click per style to design both hosts with Voice Design, and a run stops before spending anything when a seat has nothing to speak with. The picker shows free plans only what they can use.
- **Better picks, heard in your language**: "For you" keeps only voices at the normal credit price, with a notice period and without live moderation, and puts voices verified in the seat's language on Eleven v4 first; library search sits in the same tab (one fewer tab). Previews play in the seat's language when ElevenLabs has one.
- **Voices heal themselves**: when ElevenLabs no longer has a voice, the run swaps in the seat's top pick (a free plan's closest account voice; on the trial, a small vetted shelf of ElevenLabs' official voices), saves it as your choice and says so in one line under the episode's title. `npm run check:voices` and a weekly workflow check that every default and shelf voice still speaks.
- **Restricted ElevenLabs keys work**: a key without `models_read` or `user_read` is accepted (Eleven v4 is used, plan and quota are skipped), and a missing permission at generation time says which one to turn on.
- **Rate limits**: the official deployment rate-limits work routes, the analytics collector and admin sign-in per IP with Vercel Firewall rules (see the [operations guide](docs/OPERATIONS.md#rate-limits)). The app tells people to wait a minute instead of showing a raw error, and doesn't retry a rate-limited voice chunk.
- `/api/ingest` answers `400` instead of `500` when no link is given.
- **SSRF guard checks addresses**: links and custom model endpoints are refused when their host name resolves to a private, loopback, link-local or other special-purpose address, including IPv6 forms such as `::ffff:127.0.0.1`. Checked again on every redirect.
- **Admin sign-in uses scrypt**: the password check and the session signing key are derived with scrypt instead of a plain hash. Existing admin sessions sign out once.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-10-29

- **Real from the first click**: visitors without keys get a free trial that makes real episodes, with Claude Opus 5.5 and ElevenLabs Eleven v4 running on Vercel AI Gateway under the deployment's own identity. Every call is metered at its real cost and capped per visitor and per site each day; past the cap, people are asked for their own keys. The silent demo remains only where no trial is offered.
- **Admin shows real spend**: trial spend today against the cap, the live AI Gateway balance, model vs. voice cost; generation is split by compute (trial, own keys, demo); the funnel counts trial starts.
- **Sturdier voices on the trial**: transient Gateway errors are retried per line, a failed chunk is retried once, and a line that is only a delivery tag becomes a short pause.
- **Ready for the public**: an [operations guide](docs/OPERATIONS.md) for Vercel, AI Gateway, ElevenLabs and Neon (costs measured in production, limits, runbook); screenshots and the walkthrough now show real episodes; CI runs on Node 22 and 24; TypeScript 7.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-29

- **Bring anything**: Markdown, HTML, Word, PowerPoint (with speaker notes), EPUB, notebooks, subtitles, code and data files; several files, whole folders and ZIP archives become one source; several links at once; GitHub repos/files and public Google Docs via their plain‑text forms; WeChat articles; rich paste keeps headings and lists. Claude Artifact, Notion and Feishu share links get precise guidance instead of a vague failure. Everything is parsed on the device.
- **First‑party analytics** (only when the deployment has a database): anonymous events for every screen and click, sources with full referrer URLs and `utm_*`, imports, generation and listening milestones. Honours Do Not Track / GPC; opt‑out in Settings; never keys, content, file names or IPs.
- **Admin console** on its own host (`ADMIN_HOST`) or `/admin`: overview against the previous period, ordered funnel with time to first episode, sources and campaigns with conversion (click to segment), imports, clicks, generation, listening, audience, client errors, live stream, read‑only SQL and CSV/NDJSON export. Any Postgres; daily retention cron.
- **Subtraction**: the language switch left the top bar (the browser's language is used; Settings keeps it), niche TTS knobs left Settings, the occasional "try another angle" chip is gone.
- Security headers on every response.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-28

First public release candidate.

- **Two keys, zero setup**: paste an LLM key and an ElevenLabs key; providers are detected by prefix and the newest models are picked automatically (Claude Opus 5.5, Eleven v4 — future `eleven_vN` adopted without code changes). Demo mode needs no keys.
- **Two-layer recursive self-improvement**: content harness evolved HarnessPro-style (fixed label-free tests, cost/balanced/quality planners, Pareto selection with the paper's (1,2)/(1,1)/(2,1) weights → lite/medium/heavy); interface harness evolves prefill, disclosure, shortcuts and tone by replaying your past choices, with an auto-revert guard.
- **Listener portrait**: a nine-piece puzzle that fills in as you listen; editable preferences; pin any field; exploration quota.
- **ElevenLabs, fully used**: Text-to-Dialogue v4 with per-line timestamps (synced transcript) and cross-chunk continuity, per-seat voice recommendations, Voice Library search, Voice Design, Instant Voice Cloning, Eleven Music intro sting.
- **Everywhere**: Vercel web app, installable PWA, and a native-feeling macOS app that runs the whole API locally.
- **Apple-grade UI**: system colours, √φ type scale, Fibonacci spacing, golden-ratio layout, materials, springs, cursor spotlight, light/dark, reduced motion/transparency.
